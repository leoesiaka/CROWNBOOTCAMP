Exam-day techniques

Small habits that make a big difference to your score.

1

Read the whole question

Finish the stem and every option before you choose. Rushing is the easiest way to lose marks.

2

Spot the key words

Words like FIRST, BEST and MOST change what the question is really asking.

3

Think like an auditor

Prefer risk-based, business-aligned answers over purely technical fixes.

4

Eliminate first

Remove clearly wrong options, then compare what is left against the question.

5

Choose the best answer

More than one option can be right. Pick the one that fits the scenario best.

6

Stay calm and steady

Do not dwell on one hard question. Make your best choice and move on.
